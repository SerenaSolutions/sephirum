What it is

An admission gate between you and the machine: a necessity compiler decides — offline, with exact arithmetic — whether a computation is needed at all. Every decision emits an independently verifiable certificate; every refusal says why. Only the residual reaches the hardware. The hardware testifies. The certificate judges.
