# Acronym Audit — ZQL / "Zykel" (2026-10-08)

**Method:** exhaustive web sweep (Google: 6 targeted queries covering
"ZQL", "Zykel", software products, GitHub, USPTO marks, domains).
Evidence level B for findings; conclusion level D (interpretation).
This audit SUPERSEDES the offline sweep of 2026-10-07, which had
falsely indicated "ZQL" was free — lesson recorded: an offline sweep
is insufficient for brand decisions.

## Findings: ZQL (the acronym) — TAKEN

1. **SmartBear Zephyr "ZQL"** — search language of Zephyr Enterprise
   (active commercial product). Critical: "Zephyr" is a near-homonym
   of "Zephirum", same acronym, same concept (query language).
2. **Zero (rocicorp) "ZQL"** — active open-source query language.
3. **genzql** (PyPI) and **gibello/zql** (Java SQL parser) — occupy
   the open-source ecosystem.
4. Academic usage: "ZQL: privacy-preserving data processing"
   (Microsoft Research), Zenvisage ZQL (arXiv 1604.03583), ZStack
   GET-ZQL.
5. USPTO: "ZQL" registered for FURNITURE (different Nice class; does
   not block software, but the acronym space is not clean).
6. ZQL.io listed for sale on a marketplace (signal of demand).

## Findings: "Zykel" (the pronunciation) — VIABLE

1. Electronic music producer (SoundCloud) — entertainment category.
2. ZYKEL 1 Transportation LLC (Ohio, USA) — transportation category.
3. A WoW character; a Hudl sports profile — irrelevant.
4. "Zykel" = "cycles" in German mathematics (1981 paper on Minkowski
   planes) — obscure, does not block, fact recorded.
5. **Zyxel** (networking vendor) — near-homophone; attention in
   spoken/audio contexts.

## Consolidated decision

- **Registrable central mark: ZEPHIRUM** (INPI filing pending — owner
  action).
- **Official pronunciation of the acronym: "Zykel"** — viable in
  software.
- **"ZQL" bare: NOT adopted as a mark** — live collision with
  SmartBear's Zephyr in the same conceptual niche. Internal use only
  when fully qualified: "Zephirum Query Language (ZQL)".
- **Definitive check:** free public trademark search at the INPI
  portal — the legal basis is the registration, not the search engine.

## Addendum: ZYQL (2026-10-08, web sweep, 3 queries)

Candidate proposed by the owner to replace the rejected "ZQL".

Findings:
1. "ZYQL" = abbreviation of the Chinese liquor Zhuyeqing in scientific
   papers (biology, quality control) — different field, academia.
2. npm package "zyql-code" (echo-aq, v0.18.6) — small hobby project,
   NOT a query language; the only relevant software presence.
3. "Zyql" brand running Facebook ads (zyql.space, last seen Mar/2026)
   — unknown field, small.
4. zyql.cn — Chinese refrigeration factory; PUBG clan tags; OCR noise
   in old Polish documents — irrelevant.
5. No programming language, no major software product, no registered
   technology trademark located.

**Verdict (level D):** ZYQL is substantially cleaner than ZQL in the
query-language niche; collisions found are small and in other fields.
Proposed official spelling: ZYQL; pronunciation: "Zykel"; registrable
central mark: ZEPHIRUM. The definitive check remains the free public
INPI search.

## Honest limits (§12)

Search-engine audit, not legal counsel, and not a targeted search of
INPI/EUIPO/USPTO by Nice class. Does not replace a trademark
attorney's opinion. A prior-art search at the INPI is recommended
before filing (already planned in the owner's roadmap).
